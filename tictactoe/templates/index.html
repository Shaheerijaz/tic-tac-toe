How does it work?

Minimax explores possible futures: the computer maximizes its score and assumes you minimize it. Alpha and beta track the best guarantees for each player. When a branch cannot improve the decision, the search skips the remaining alternatives.

A win scores 10 minus its depth, a loss scores depth minus 10, and a draw scores 0. This favors quicker wins and delays unavoidable losses. Cutoffs count pruning events, not skipped nodes. Statistics describe the last computer move.
